One image that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026's world news: a briefing poster of the day's seven most consequential developments as the poster presents them (the Russia-Ukraine grind under record drone and missile strikes; Iran's endgame, with US strikes, a near-total internet blackout and a leadership in hiding; US midterm aftershocks, with Congress certifying results and Trump's approval at 36 percent; the AI trade unwinding, Nvidia down pre-earnings, a hawkish Fed hold, gold through $4,700 and oil sliding; Europe's defence surge toward 3.5 percent of GDP by 2028; Bangladesh's dengue emergency past 90,000 cases; and a quantum-internet milestone, entanglement distributed over a 1,200 km satellite link), plus an also-moving sidebar (China-Taiwan coast-guard shadowing near Kinmen, famine declared in Sudan's El Fasher, COP31's finance text still blocked, crypto ETF outflows at a record) and a day watchlist (the Fed minutes and US CPI, the Shenzhen trilateral communiqué, Nvidia earnings after the bell, and the WHO dengue situation report). `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies the poster's JPEG bytes, held in the repository as `entry.jpg` (1024 by 683 pixels, 359,353 bytes), to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# writes out/entry.png, the same bytes on every run
```
