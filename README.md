The artwork is a one-page briefing poster for Wednesday 18 November
2026, built around seven developments from that day. Iran aimed at
bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, air defences intercepted the attack and
no casualties were reported. In Bangladesh the garment wage protests
reached day 19, with the Dhaka-Mymensingh highway blocked in Gazipur,
a police vehicle torched, and clashes in Mirpur. Xi and Putin met for
over two hours in Shenzhen, and a Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral was set
for November 19. On the economy, UK inflation printed 3.6 percent and
euro-area inflation 4.0 percent, while Bank Indonesia lifted its rate
by 25 basis points to 6.00 percent. The FAA froze Juniper router
installations, leaving 68 units disabled, as a $50.1bn modernisation
request sat before Congress. Nvidia closed the leadership question,
its board ruling out a return for Huang after third-quarter revenue
of $115.8bn beat a $109.1bn consensus, with his resignation effective
November 12. Australia sanctioned three individuals and four
organisations over West Bank settlement expansion and settler
violence.

At the centre of the poster a market board tracks the Strait of
Hormuz, Brent crude at $87.40, up 2.8 percent, and Asian LNG at
$29.10, up 2.4 percent. A follow-ups strip along the bottom records
four running stories: the Kerch Bridge road-arch collapse, verified
on November 18 from the November 17 strike, with rail traffic since
resumed; Cyclone Akvan, due to make landfall by noon on November 20;
the WHO Bundibugyo Emergency Committee, which met but had published
no PHEIC decision by the time the poster was compiled; and an
Amnesty report counting nine RSF drone strikes that killed 37
civilians around El Obeid between June and August.

The program in this repository copies the poster JPEG held as
entry.jpg to /output/entry.png, byte-identical on every run.

## Run it

```bash
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
